Software solutions for businesses in Kuwait

ERP, mobile app and website development planning for companies serving Kuwait, covering bilingual interfaces, business integrations and acceptance testing.

Decisions to make before development

Settling these points early makes the scope, testing and handover easier to review.

Customer and staff tasks

Specify the booking, purchase or approval task that the product must support. Test Arabic and English content with representative users before extending the interface.

Systems and providers

Review accounting connections, payment-provider contracts and device requirements. Verify current local obligations with the responsible advisers instead of relying on a generic software claim.

Acceptance and handover

Use a sample dataset to test complete workflows and exceptions. Agree on source-code rights, deployment documentation, monitoring and support terms in writing.
