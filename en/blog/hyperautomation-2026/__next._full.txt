<h3>What is Hyperautomation?</h3>
<p>Hyperautomation combines multiple technologies:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Robotic Process Automation (RPA):</strong> Bots that mimic human actions in software</li>
<li><strong>Artificial Intelligence:</strong> Decision-making and pattern recognition</li>
<li><strong>Machine Learning:</strong> Self-improving algorithms</li>
<li><strong>Process Mining:</strong> Discovering automation opportunities</li>
<li><strong>Low-Code/No-Code:</strong> Citizen developers building automations</li>
</ul>
