d:T8c1,<h2>The Rise of Digital Wallets</h2>
<p>Digital wallets have become the most popular payment method globally, accounting for over 50% of e-commerce transactions. From Apple Pay to local solutions, consumers expect seamless, secure payment experiences.</p>
<h3>Types of Digital Wallets</h3>
<p><strong>1. Mobile Wallets</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay</li>
<li>NFC contactless payments</li>
<li>Integrated with device biometrics</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>2. Crypto Wallets</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Non-custodial (self-custody)</li>
<li>Custodial (exchange-managed)</li>
<li>Multi-chain support</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>3. Bank Wallets</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Direct bank account access</li>
<li>Instant transfers</li>
<li>Bill payments and subscriptions</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>4. Super Apps</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>All-in-one financial management</li>
<li>Payments, savings, investments</li>
<li>Popular in Asia and Africa</li>
</ul>
<h3>Payment Gateway Integration</h3>
<p>A payment gateway is the technology that captures and transfers payment data between customers, merchants, and banks.</p>
<p><strong>Key Features:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Multi-currency support</li>
<li>Fraud detection</li>
<li>PCI DSS compliance</li>
<li>Real-time settlement</li>
</ul>
