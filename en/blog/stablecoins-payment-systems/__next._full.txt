21:T7c6,<h2>What Are Stablecoins?</h2>
<p>Stablecoins are cryptocurrencies designed to maintain a stable value by being pegged to a reserve asset, typically the US Dollar. Unlike Bitcoin or Ethereum, stablecoins don't experience wild price swings, making them ideal for payments and commerce.</p>
<h3>The Big Players</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>USDT (Tether):</strong> The largest stablecoin by market cap, widely used in trading and payments</li>
<li><strong>USDC (USD Coin):</strong> Backed by Coinbase and Circle, known for regulatory compliance</li>
<li><strong>PYUSD:</strong> PayPal's entry into the stablecoin market</li>
</ul>
<h3>Why Businesses Are Adopting Stablecoins</h3>
<ol>
<li><strong>Instant Settlements:</strong> No more waiting 3-5 days for international transfers</li>
<li><strong>Lower Fees:</strong> Transaction costs as low as $0.001 on efficient networks</li>
<li><strong>24/7 Availability:</strong> Unlike banks, crypto networks never close</li>
<li><strong>Global Reach:</strong> Send money anywhere without currency conversion hassles</li>
</ol>
<h3>Stablecoins on Solana</h3>
<p>The Solana blockchain has emerged as a preferred network for stablecoin transactions:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Sub-second finality</strong> - Transactions confirm in under 1 second</li>
<li><strong>Ultra-low fees</strong> - Fractions of a cent per transaction</li>
<li><strong>USDC native support</strong> - Circle has official USDC on Solana</li>
<li><strong>Visa &#x26; PayPal integration</strong> - Major companies settling on Solana</li>
</ul>
<h3>How Smart Team Implements Stablecoin Payments</h3>
<p>We help businesses integrate stablecoin payment solutions:</p>
<ul>
<li>Custom payment gateways accepting USDT/USDC</li>
<li>Multi-chain wallet integration (Solana, Ethereum, Polygon)</li>
<li>Real-time conversion to local currencies</li>
<li>Compliance-ready reporting systems</li>
</ul>
