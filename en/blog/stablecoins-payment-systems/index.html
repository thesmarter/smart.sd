Stablecoins in Payment Systems: USDT, USDC & The Future of Money

Prepared by the Smart Team editorial team · Editorial review: November 2, 2026

What does this article explain?

Stablecoins aim to track a reference asset but retain issuer, redemption and network risks. Review these factors before planning a payment integration.

What Are Stablecoins?

Stablecoins are cryptocurrencies designed to maintain a stable value by being pegged to a reserve asset, typically the US Dollar. Unlike Bitcoin or Ethereum, stablecoins don't experience wild price swings, making them ideal for payments and commerce.

The Big Players

  • USDT (Tether): The largest stablecoin by market cap, widely used in trading and payments
  • USDC (USD Coin): Backed by Coinbase and Circle, known for regulatory compliance
  • PYUSD: PayPal's entry into the stablecoin market

Why Businesses Are Adopting Stablecoins

  1. Instant Settlements: No more waiting 3-5 days for international transfers
  2. Lower Fees: Transaction costs as low as $0.001 on efficient networks
  3. 24/7 Availability: Unlike banks, crypto networks never close
  4. Global Reach: Send money anywhere without currency conversion hassles

Stablecoins on Solana

The Solana blockchain has emerged as a preferred network for stablecoin transactions:

  • Sub-second finality - Transactions confirm in under 1 second
  • Ultra-low fees - Fractions of a cent per transaction
  • USDC native support - Circle has official USDC on Solana
  • Visa & PayPal integration - Major companies settling on Solana
