<h3>1. Security: Sleep Better at Night</h3>
<p>WordPress is the #1 target for hackers globally because of its plugin-based architecture. A single outdated plugin can compromise your entire business data. <strong>Next.js</strong> provides a "Static" frontend, meaning there is no database exposed to the public web. It is effectively unhackable by traditional means.</p>
<h3>2. Performance: Winning the SEO Race</h3>
<p>Google’s <strong>Core Web Vitals</strong> are the secret to ranking #1. WordPress sites often struggle with "Largest Contentful Paint" due to heavy themes and database queries. Next.js pre-renders every page, serving content at lightning speed from global Edge servers. Your site will load before your competitor’s even starts to render.</p>
<h3>3. Total Creative Freedom</h3>
<p>WordPress traps you in "Themes." If you want a unique feature, you usually have to settle for a "good enough" plugin. With Next.js, we build exactly what your brand needs—no compromises, no limits.</p>
