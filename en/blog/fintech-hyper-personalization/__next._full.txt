<h3>What is Hyper-Personalization?</h3>
<p>Unlike traditional personalization (which might just put your name in an email), hyper-personalization uses AI and real-time data to provide financial insights and services that are unique to your specific context.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Smart Saving Goals:</strong> AI that knows when you have extra cash and automatically rounds up for your dream vacation.</li>
<li><strong>Proactive Fraud Alerts:</strong> Security that understands your travel habits better than you do.</li>
<li><strong>Instant Credit Decisions:</strong> Credit scoring based on 360-degree behavioral data, not just a static history.</li>
</ul>
