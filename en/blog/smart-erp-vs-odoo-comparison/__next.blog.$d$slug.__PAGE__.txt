<h3>1. The "Module Trap" vs. The "Integrated Power"</h3>
<p>Odoo's greatest strength is also its weakness. To get a fully functional system, you often need to install and pay for dozens of individual modules. <strong>Smart ERP</strong> comes with all essential Enterprise features (HR, Accounting, Supply Chain) pre-integrated and optimized for regional workflows. You don't have to worry about inter-module compatibility.</p>
<h3>2. Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)</h3>
<p>With Odoo, the initial license might seem attractive, but the costs of "Implementation Partners" and specific localizations can triple the price. Smart Team provides a direct relationship, meaning your investment goes into the product and its success, not into middleman consulting fees.</p>
<h2>Conclusion: Which is for you?</h2>
<p>Choose <strong>Odoo</strong> if you have a massive internal IT team ready to handle constant updates and configurations.
