22:T1f21,<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p><img src="/logos/n8n-logo.png" alt="n8n Logo"></p>
<p>Automation is no longer a luxury—it is a foundational requirement for modern digital businesses. As organizations scale, they rely on dozens of tools: CRMs, ERPs, payment gateways, analytics platforms, communication apps, and AI services. The real challenge is not adopting these tools, but <strong>orchestrating them into a single, reliable operational flow</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>n8n</strong> is an open‑source workflow automation platform designed to solve this exact problem. It enables engineering teams, operations teams, and enterprises to design complex automations with full control over logic, execution, and—most importantly—data ownership.</p>
<p>Unlike no‑code SaaS automation tools that lock you into their infrastructure, n8n gives you the freedom to self‑host, customize, extend, and deeply integrate automation into your existing architecture.</p>
<h2>What Is n8n?</h2>
<p>n8n (short for <em>nodemation</em>) is a <strong>node‑based automation engine</strong> that allows you to visually design workflows connecting APIs, databases, internal services, and third‑party platforms.</p>
<p>Each workflow is composed of interconnected nodes, where each node performs a specific function such as triggering events, executing business logic, transforming data, or communicating with external systems.</p>
<h3>Core Capabilities</h3>
<ul>
<li>Fully open‑source (fair‑code license)</li>
<li>Visual workflow builder</li>
<li>Native JavaScript execution</li>
<li>Advanced branching and error handling</li>
<li>REST, GraphQL, Webhooks, and custom APIs</li>
<li>Over 400 built‑in integrations</li>
</ul>

<h2>How n8n Works (Deep Dive)</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Trigger Execution</strong> – A workflow starts via webhook, schedule, or event</li>
<li><strong>Context Propagation</strong> – JSON data flows between nodes</li>
<li><strong>Logic Processing</strong> – Conditions, loops, and expressions modify execution paths</li>
<li><strong>Error Strategies</strong> – Retry, fallback, or continue‑on‑fail</li>
<li><strong>Persistence</strong> – Execution data stored for audit and debugging</li>
</ol>
<p>This execution model allows deterministic, traceable automations—critical for regulated environments.</p>
