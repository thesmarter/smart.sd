21:T978,<h2>What is an ERP System?</h2>
<p>Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) is integrated software that manages all core business processes in real-time. From finance and HR to inventory and sales, ERP provides a single source of truth for your entire organization.</p>
<h3>Core ERP Modules</h3>
<p><strong>1. Financial Management</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>General ledger and accounting</li>
<li>Accounts payable/receivable</li>
<li>Budgeting and forecasting</li>
<li>Financial reporting and compliance</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>2. Human Resources</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Employee records and payroll</li>
<li>Recruitment and onboarding</li>
<li>Performance management</li>
<li>Benefits administration</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>3. Supply Chain Management</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Inventory tracking</li>
<li>Procurement and purchasing</li>
<li>Warehouse management</li>
<li>Supplier relationship management</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>4. Sales &#x26; CRM</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Customer database</li>
<li>Order management</li>
<li>Sales pipeline</li>
<li>Marketing automation</li>
</ul>

<h3>Cloud vs On-Premise ERP</h3>
<p><strong>Cloud ERP (Recommended):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Lower upfront costs</li>
<li>Automatic updates</li>
<li>Access from anywhere</li>
<li>Faster deployment</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>On-Premise ERP:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Full data control</li>
<li>Customization flexibility</li>
<li>No internet dependency</li>
<li>Higher security (for some industries)</li>
</ul>
