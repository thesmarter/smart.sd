n8n and Zapier: hosting, integrations and cost

Prepared by the Smart Team editorial team · Editorial review: November 2, 2026

What does this article explain?

Compare n8n and Zapier through supported connectors, hosting, licensing, task volume and maintenance. Self-hosting still needs infrastructure and operational work.

Introduction: The "Task Tax" Problem

Automation is the backbone of the modern enterprise. But most companies start with Zapier only to realize that as they grow, their monthly bill explodes. Zapier charges you "per task"—meaning the more successful your business becomes, the more they tax you.

At Smart Team, we implement n8n—a Fair-Code workflow automation tool that puts you back in control. Here is why we believe n8n wins the comparison for any serious technical implementation.

The Automation ROI

Feature Smart Team (n8n Solution) Zapier (SaaS Model)
Pricing Fixed Hosting Cost Per Task/Zap (Expensive at scale)
Data Privacy On-premise (Your Servers) Cloud-only (Third-party servers)
Logic Complexity Advanced (Binary, JS, Loops) Basic (Linear workflows)
Task Limit Unlimited Strictly limited by plan
Integration Custom API & Database level Pre-made app connectors only

1. Unlimited Growth, Zero Extra Cost

In Zapier, if you automate 50,000 tasks, you could pay hundreds of dollars a month. With an n8n instance deployed by Smart Team on your own servers, you can run 50,000 or 5,000,000 tasks for the same low hosting fee. We remove the ceiling on your business growth.

2. Radical Data Sovereignty

Financial and Enterprise data shouldn't live on 3rd-party cloud platforms if you can help it. Because n8n is self-hosted, your data stays within your firewall. This makes it the only choice for banks, government entities, and high-security startups in the region.

3. Professional Grade Power

Zapier is great for simple "If This, Then That" logic. But real business needs complex data transformations, JavaScript execution, and deep database integrations. Smart Team leverages n8n's "Node-based" interface to build complex, multi-branched workflows that Zapier simply cannot handle.

The Conclusion

Zapier is a toy for small tasks; n8n is a tool for building an automated empire. If you want to own your workflows, protect your data, and save thousands of dollars while scaling, you need a Smart Automation strategy.
