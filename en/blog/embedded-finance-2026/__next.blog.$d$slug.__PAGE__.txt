d:Tf20,<h2>Finance Goes Invisible</h2>
<p>By 2026, banking will become invisible. You won't need to open a banking app to access financial services—they'll be seamlessly woven into the apps you already use. This is <strong>Embedded Finance</strong>: the integration of financial services into non-financial platforms.</p>
<h3>The Embedded Finance Revolution</h3>
<p><strong>What's Changing:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>E-commerce:</strong> Buy now, pay later at checkout on any platform</li>
<li><strong>Ride-sharing:</strong> Driver financing and instant payouts within the app</li>
<li><strong>Healthcare:</strong> Patient payment plans embedded in medical portals</li>
<li><strong>HR Software:</strong> Salary advances and benefits within payroll systems</li>
</ul>
