Custom payment software and Stripe integration

Prepared by the Smart Team editorial team · Editorial review: November 2, 2026

What does this article explain?

Custom payment software can integrate with a provider such as Stripe where available. Review merchant eligibility, payment methods, fees and reconciliation first.

Introduction

When it comes to online payments, Stripe is the global gold standard. However, for many businesses in the MENA region—especially in Sudan and parts of the GCC—using Stripe is either restricted or technically challenging due to local banking limitations.

Smart Payment by Smart Team was designed to bridge this gap, providing a "Stripe-like" experience with 100% regional compliance and local payment support.

Key Differences

Feature Smart Payment Stripe
Regional Availability Full support in Sudan, KSA, UAE Restricted in many African countries
Local Methods Bok, MyCashi, EBS, Mada, SyberPay, Fawry, Local Cards Mainly International Cards
Currency Support SDG, SAR, AED, USD Primarily USD, EUR, GBP
KYC/Compliance Tailored to local regulations Global standards (often mismatched)
Settlement To local bank accounts Requires supported offshore accounts

1. Breaking the Border Barriers

For a business in Sudan or even a growing startup in Saudi Arabia, getting a verified Stripe account can be a nightmare of paperwork and offshore incorporation. Smart Payment offers a localized onboarding process that understands the requirements of local business licenses, allowing you to start accepting payments in days, not months.

2. Local Payment Methods (The Real Game Changer)

Stripe excels at Credit Cards (Visa/Mastercard). But in our region, customers often prefer local methods like SyberPay, MyCashi, EBS, Mada, Fawry, Local Cards in Sudan or Mada, Sadad, Paymob in KSA. Smart Payment integrates these directly into your checkout page, drastically reducing cart abandonment and increasing consumer trust.

The Verdict

If you are a global enterprise targeting the US and Europe, Stripe is excellent. But if your mission is to dominate the local and regional markets, Smart Payment provides the tools, currency support, and banking integrations you actually need to succeed.
