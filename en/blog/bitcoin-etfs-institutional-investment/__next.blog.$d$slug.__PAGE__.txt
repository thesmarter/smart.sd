d:T8ee,<h2>The Bitcoin ETF Revolution</h2>
<p>2024 marked a historic turning point for cryptocurrency with the SEC's approval of spot Bitcoin ETFs in January. For the first time, institutional investors can gain Bitcoin exposure through traditional brokerage accounts.</p>
<h3>What is a Bitcoin ETF?</h3>
<p>An Exchange-Traded Fund (ETF) is a security that tracks an asset's price and can be bought/sold on stock exchanges like regular stocks. A Bitcoin ETF:</p>
<ul>
<li>Holds actual Bitcoin (spot ETF) or Bitcoin futures</li>
<li>Trades on regulated exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ)</li>
<li>Eliminates need for crypto wallets or exchanges</li>
<li>Provides regulatory protection for investors</li>
</ul>
