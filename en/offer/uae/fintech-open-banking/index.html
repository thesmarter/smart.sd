Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Open Banking and investment opportunities in the UAE

Have another question? Contact us.
What is Open Banking?

It is a financial practice that allows third-party service providers (like FinTechs) secure access to customer financial data via APIs, enabling them to offer innovative financial services.

What is the role of the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE)?

CBUAE launched the 'Financial Infrastructure Transformation' (FIT) program in 2023, with 'Open Finance' as a core pillar to create an integrated digital financial environment.

What are the benefits for consumers in the UAE?

A unified view and management of accounts from different banks in one app, accurate financial analytics, and easier access to loans based on verified financial data.

How do UAE businesses benefit?

Automation of financial processes, improved cash flow and liquidity management, and innovative, lower-cost payment solutions through direct bank integration.

Is the system secure according to UAE standards?

Encryption, permissions, logs and integrations need security review before operation. An MVP is not security certification or regulatory approval.

What is the situation in ADGM and DIFC?

These financial centers are leaders in developing advanced Open Banking regulatory frameworks, providing an ideal environment for FinTech growth.

What licenses are required in the UAE?

Licensing depends on the activity, operating location and regulator. Applicable CBUAE Open Finance requirements are not automatically replaced by ADGM or DIFC permissions. Review current rules with qualified advisers.

Are there consumer fees?

Basic services are usually free, but companies may offer advanced features for a nominal subscription fee, which must be fully disclosed before activation.

How does Open Banking contribute to UAE Vision 2031?

It is an integral part of the national digital transformation strategy, enhancing the UAE's position as a global hub for the digital economy and financial innovation.

Can I revoke data sharing permissions at any time?

Yes, customers have full authority to grant and revoke consent instantly through their bank app or an authorized service provider's app.

Which data privacy protection laws are in force?

Operations are subject to the Federal Decree-Law on Personal Data Protection, as well as regulations and standards issued by the Central Bank of the UAE.
