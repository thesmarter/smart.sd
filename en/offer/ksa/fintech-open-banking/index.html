Saudi FinTech investment opportunity in digital payments and open banking

A financial software platform available for acquisition or licensing by banks, fintech companies and payment providers. The offer covers account aggregation, expense analysis and budgeting interfaces, subject to independent integration, security and SAMA requirements review before operation.

What is the investment opportunity?

This is an opportunity tied to an existing software product, not a promise of returns or a ready financial licence.

The opportunity is intended for an investor or company that wants to shorten part of the product-build phase for the Saudi market. The asset offered is the urCASH MVP, including mobile interfaces, an administration panel and a customizable scope. The transaction should start with a review of the code, rights and integration status, followed by a decision on what can be reused and what needs new development.

The product can support digital-payment, open-banking and financial-data use cases. Working with Saudi banks, accessing account data or initiating payments requires a separate regulatory and contractual route. The software transaction and the authorization to operate a financial service must therefore be assessed separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Open Banking and investment opportunities

Have another question? Contact us.
What is Open Banking?

It is a financial practice that allows third-party service providers (like FinTech companies) secure access to customer financial data (with their explicit consent) via APIs. It aims to empower customers to share their banking data for innovative financial services.

What is the regulatory status of Open Banking in Saudi Arabia (SAMA)?

SAMA launched the 'Open Banking Policy' in 2021 and released the 'Open Banking Framework' in November 2022. The framework includes rules, regulations, and technical standards for banks and FinTech companies.

What are the benefits for individual consumers?

A comprehensive view of all bank accounts in one app, automated expense analysis and budgeting tools, and easier data sharing for faster and better credit decisions.

How do businesses and institutions benefit?

Automation of Accounting processes, consolidated account data for better investment decisions, and enabling direct account-to-account payments at lower costs than traditional cards.

Is sharing financial data secure?

Yes, Open Banking in Saudi Arabia relies on strict security standards. Data is encrypted and shared only via approved APIs. All authorized companies are supervised by SAMA.

How can I control my data sharing permissions?

Consent is granted explicitly for the specific service you want. You can revoke this consent at any time through your bank app or the service provider's app instantly.

What licenses are required to provide these services?

Companies must obtain specific licenses from SAMA, such as Account Information Service Provider (AISP) for data viewing, or Payment Initiation Service Provider (PISP) for starting payments.

Are there fees for using Open Banking services?

Mostly, basic services provided via banks are free for consumers. FinTech companies may charge for advanced features, but all fees must be disclosed upfront.

How does Open Banking contribute to Vision 2030?

It is a key pillar of the 'Financial Sector Development Program' under Saudi Vision 2030, aiming to make the Kingdom a global FinTech hub and boost financial innovation.

Is using Open Banking services mandatory?

No, it is completely optional. The customer is the sole decider in starting these services or sticking with traditional banking without sharing data.

Which laws protect data privacy?

Review the Personal Data Protection Law and SAMA requirements applicable to the activity, including processing purpose, consent, retention, permissions and data sharing.

How does our proposed system support entry into the Saudi market?

Review the MVP to identify reusable components and integration status. Independent technical and regulatory assessment is required; buying software does not guarantee authorization or its timing.
