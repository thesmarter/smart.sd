Fintech & Payment Solutions

Payment and open banking software integrations with transaction tracking, reconciliation and access controls, scoped around your provider and market requirements.

What the financial software service covers

We develop interfaces and integrations for payments, transaction records and financial workflows. The agreed scope identifies the payment provider, supported operations, currencies and reporting needs before implementation.

Payment integration

An integration must handle successful, pending and failed transactions. We review webhook verification, duplicate requests, refunds and reconciliation against provider records. Sensitive payment data should use the provider's supported handling method.

Open banking and permissions

Open banking uses approved access to account data or payment initiation. Consent, permissions and the provider's available APIs shape the implementation. A software integration does not itself grant permission to offer a regulated financial service.

Market review before launch

Saudi and UAE projects need a review of the operator's role, provider contracts and applicable approvals. See the SAMA open banking framework and the CBUAE licensing requirements. Technical acceptance and regulatory authorization are separate checks.

Questions about this service

Does the software include a financial services licence?

No. Software development or licensing is separate from the operator's regulatory permissions. The required authorization depends on the activity and market.

Can you integrate our selected payment provider?

We review the provider's supported APIs, account approval, environments and required operations before confirming integration scope.
