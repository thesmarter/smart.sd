Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)

ERP software connecting accounting, sales, inventory and HR. Smart Team maps your processes, configures modules and plans migration and integrations.

What does an ERP system do?

An ERP system gives departments a shared record of transactions, stock and business activity. A sale can update inventory and accounting without asking staff to enter the same information in separate systems.

What we develop

We define the modules your business needs: accounting, purchasing, sales, inventory, customer management or HR. Roles, approval rules and reports follow your actual operating process. We also review whether Adaa ERP fits the scope before recommending custom development.

How implementation works

We start with current processes and sample data, agree on the first release, then configure or develop the required modules. Migration includes field mapping, data cleaning and reconciliation. A limited pilot helps staff test the workflow before a wider rollout.

What to prepare

Share your branches, users, current tools, reporting needs and any accounting or tax requirements. Existing data and integrations affect the delivery plan. Training, support and ownership of custom work belong in the agreed scope.

Questions about this service

Can ERP connect to existing applications?

Yes, where the existing application exposes a supported API or export format. We review access, field mapping and reconciliation before including an integration in scope.

Do we need every ERP module at launch?

No. A first release can cover the most important workflow, followed by additional modules after data and staff processes are tested.

What determines ERP implementation cost?

Modules, users, branches, data migration, integrations and custom workflows determine the scope. We estimate cost after reviewing these requirements.
