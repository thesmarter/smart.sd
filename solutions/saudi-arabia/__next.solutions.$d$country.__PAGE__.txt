An AI agent combines a language model with defined tools and instructions. It can retrieve information or prepare an action in a business system. Its access should be limited to the tasks you approve.

Examples include finding answers in company documents, preparing CRM updates, classifying incoming requests and drafting follow-ups. We start with a bounded task whose output can be checked against examples and known records.

6:Tb86,## What does an ERP system do?
An ERP system gives departments a shared record of transactions, stock and business activity. A sale can update inventory and accounting without asking staff to enter the same information in separate systems.

## What we develop
We define the modules your business needs: accounting, purchasing, sales, inventory, customer management or HR. Roles, approval rules and reports follow your actual operating process. We also review whether [Adaa ERP](/adaa_help) fits the scope before recommending custom development.

## How implementation works
We start with current processes and sample data, agree on the first release, then configure or develop the required modules. Migration includes field mapping, data cleaning and reconciliation. A limited pilot helps staff test the workflow before a wider rollout.

## What to prepare
Share your branches, users, current tools, reporting needs and any accounting or tax requirements. Existing data and integrations affect the delivery plan. Training, support and ownership of custom work belong in the agreed scope.

7:Tca9,## What the financial software service covers
We develop interfaces and integrations for payments, transaction records and financial workflows. The agreed scope identifies the payment provider, supported operations, currencies and reporting needs before implementation.

## Payment integration
An integration must handle successful, pending and failed transactions. We review webhook verification, duplicate requests, refunds and reconciliation against provider records. Sensitive payment data should use the provider's supported handling method.

## Open banking and permissions
Open banking uses approved access to account data or payment initiation. Consent, permissions and the provider's available APIs shape the implementation. A software integration does not itself grant permission to offer a regulated financial service.

## Market review before launch
Saudi and UAE projects need a review of the operator's role, provider contracts and applicable approvals. See the [SAMA open banking framework](https://www.openbanking.sama.gov.sa/index-en.html) and the [CBUAE licensing requirements](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/article-2-licensing-and-licensing-procedures-0). Technical acceptance and regulatory authorization are separate checks.
